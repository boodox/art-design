concept: you play as a humanoid lynx that was transported through a strange portal into a different realm.
This realm was a pocket dimension for random items and creatures to be stolen and stored from other dimensions.
While in this realm, the lynx comes across weapons that end up giving him super strength and unnatural abilities to survive against anything that comes his way.
The realm you end up exploring resembles tropical forest you would see in real life but with a strange otherworldly look to it.
The lynx goes on to confront many creatures of the realm while trying to find his way home.

gameplay inspiration: I wanted the gameplay to be somewhat similar to Kirby's return to dreamland.
